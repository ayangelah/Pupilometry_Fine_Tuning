Pupil diameter through the reflex

Resting diameter is read from the first illuminated, pre-constriction frames (the reflex-latency window), then the pupil constricts to its minimum.

Detector evidence

The measurement, drawn on the eye

Blue is the iris ruler the pipeline calibrates against, a fixed size across the whole reflex. Green is the pupil measured in each frame. The pupil tightens as the reflex fires; the ruler holds constant.

Calibration

Diameter in millimeters via the iris as a per-frame ruler: pupil_mm = (pupil_px / iris_px) × 11.7. The ratio cancels distance, zoom, and angle. Percent constriction is independent of the 11.7 mm assumption.

Known limits

The 11.7 mm iris constant and off-axis foreshortening are stated error sources. Recall degrades on dark irises in dim light (visible-light vs infrared).
